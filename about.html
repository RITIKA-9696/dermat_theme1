How It Started

A studio built on patience, not rushing.

"We never wanted to be the busiest clinic in the city — only the calmest one on the street."

Dr. Aditi Verma founded Lumière Skin in 2015 after years in larger dermatology practices where she watched patients feel rushed, unheard, and often anxious about their skin. She wanted to build something different — a studio where appointments could stretch, where every skin concern was taken seriously, and where the whole atmosphere felt less clinical and more like a retreat.

Today, the studio is still intentionally small. We see fewer patients each day than most clinics our size, and we spend longer with each of them. We use advanced, medical-grade treatments — but always in a calm, considered way, and only when they genuinely serve your skin.

Our patients come for glow, acne, anti-ageing and medical skin concerns. But they stay because they feel genuinely looked after — in a space that feels as peaceful as the results look.

Dr. Aditi Verma
Founder & Lead Dermatologist
