This patient arrived with dull, uneven tone and years of sun damage from outdoor work. We built a staged plan — combining hydrafacials, gentle peels and laser resurfacing — with a personalised home routine to support the results.

  • Six hydrafacial sessions spaced 2–3 weeks apart
  • Two gentle chemical peels layered into the plan
