Before Your Visit

Common questions, gently answered.

A few things patients often ask us before their first appointment — feel free to call if you have others.

Call the studio
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking in advance so you get your preferred time slot. We do accept walk-ins for urgent concerns, but scheduled patients are seen first.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a valid ID, a list of current medications, and any previous skin reports or allergy information. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete our patient form.

Is parking available at the studio?

Yes — free parking is available in the building basement for all patients. There is also ample street parking on Lansdowne Terrace.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Typically within 1–2 working days for routine consultations. For urgent skin concerns, we offer same-day slots — please call ahead so we can reserve a time for you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — for larger treatment plans we offer bundled pricing and interest-free instalments. Ask during your consultation and we'll outline the options.

Can I have a consultation before booking treatment?

Absolutely — and we encourage it. A consultation lets us assess your skin properly and recommend what's actually right for you, with no obligation to book treatment the same day.
