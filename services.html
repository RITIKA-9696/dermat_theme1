Common Questions

Things patients often ask.

A few answers before your first visit — and if your question isn't here, we're always happy to talk it through.

Ask us anything
Which treatment should I start with?

Almost always, a consultation. We'll examine, listen and recommend a plan in order of priority — sometimes starting with something simple and gentle before more advanced treatments.

Are your treatments painful?

Most treatments are completely comfortable. Laser and microneedling may have a brief warming sensation, and we use numbing cream wherever needed. Your comfort is our priority.

Is there downtime after treatments?

Hydrafacials and most peels have no downtime. Lasers and deeper peels may involve 1–3 days of mild redness. We'll always tell you exactly what to expect beforehand.

How many sessions will I need?

This depends on your skin concern. Hydrafacials can be single-session, while acne therapy and laser resurfacing typically need a series of 3–6 sessions spaced a few weeks apart.

Do you offer packages or payment plans?

Yes — for larger treatment plans we offer bundled pricing and interest-free instalments. Ask during your consultation and we'll outline the options.

Can I wear makeup after treatment?

For most treatments we recommend waiting 12–24 hours before applying makeup, to let your skin settle. We'll give you personalised aftercare instructions after your visit.
